# Benchmark: which model writes agent-jobs code (2026-11-06)

Question: how much of the coding now done by Opus can move to cheaper models without losing quality? Target: move more than 30% of Opus coding volume.

- **Sonnet high** is close to Opus on small and medium tasks at about a quarter of the API-equivalent cost. On #119 it was the only branch the reviewer called fully fixed; Opus won on #146 and #135.
- **Sonnet medium** is cheaper again but leaves half again as many High findings as Sonnet high.
- **GLM 5.3** is not reliable as a coder here: 4 of 9 jobs failed (one timeout, three ended without a report while leaving a commit), its turn counts are not trustworthy, and its spend is real money ($5.79 on OpenRouter for the day).
- **Haiku** fixed one small task of three, with two to three times the turns.
- #154, #155 and #134 were not fully fixed by any model, Opus included.

Proposed routing: small and medium backend tasks outside money, auth, prod DB, trading and infra go to Sonnet high with the usual sol review; large tasks, architecture and the sensitive areas stay on Opus. Small and medium tasks were 57% of Opus's cost in this run; moving them at roughly a quarter of the price cuts Opus coding load by about 40%.

## Method

- **Tasks:** nine open agent-jobs issues, all from base `8f6e8f9` (origin/main), sized small, medium and large by the orchestrator before the run.
- **Models:** Opus high, Sonnet high, Sonnet medium (subscription), GLM 5.3 through the OpenRouter runner (Baidu fp8, $1.50 budget per job), Haiku high on the small tasks only. 39 coder jobs, kind `fix`, one branch each (`bench/<issue>-<model>`), local commit, no push.
- **Brief:** the issue text plus fixed rules (run `go test` and `go vet`, commit on the bench branch). Identical for every model.
- **Review:** one `gpt-6.1-sol` high review job per branch, started automatically when the coder job finished. The reviewer ends with `BENCH-VERDICT: critical= high= medium= low= issue_fixed=yes|partly|no`. Branches whose job failed but left a commit were reviewed too.
- **Cost:** Claude models in API-equivalent dollars from the job usage; GLM from OpenRouter's own figure.

## Caveats

- One run per cell: this is a signal, not statistics.
- The sol sandbox cannot run tests, so verdicts come from reading the diff.
- Five jobs ran in parallel and ran test suites at the same time, so wall times are inflated and not comparable.
- How the Claude subscription weighs Sonnet against Opus in the weekly limit is not published; the saving assumes it follows API price.
- Sonnet ended its first attempt without a report four times; agent-jobs auto-continued each one.
