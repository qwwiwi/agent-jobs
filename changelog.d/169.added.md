- Crew jobs (#169): a cheap main model with a capped advisor at design forks. `job_start` `advisor_model` (a claude model; refused for codex jobs, `gpt-*` / `openrouter:` advisors and gate kinds), `advisor_max_calls` (default 2, ceiling 5), `advisor_max_budget_usd` (default $2, ceiling $10, on top of `max_budget_usd`) and `paired_with` (CLI `--advisor-model`, `--advisor-max-calls`, `--advisor-max-budget-usd`, `--paired-with`). A crew job's MCP server offers `advisor_ask(question, context)`: through the daemon's proxy, a short read-only `claude -p` on the advisor model in the job's work tree (Read, Glob, Grep only; the daemon's own settings with write tools denied and the guard read-only on the work dir; no project settings or MCP servers; the job runner's environment; 12 turns, 5 minutes, an equal share of the crew budget left over the calls left; never started once the job has left `running`, and ended by its cancel, stop, timeout or finish). One call at a time; the caps are claimed in one transaction before the model runs; each call is recorded (turn, question, outcome, turns, cost; a call without a reported cost, or with a missing, null or negative one, counts at its whole budget; claude's budget is checked between turns, so a call may overshoot by one advisor turn). `job_status` / `job_result` show `crew` with the calls as a timeline and the cost split main / advisor (main over all attempts, `cost_main_unknown` when one reported no cost); `job_stats` lists `crew_pairs` (a job next to the job it names in `paired_with`: model, advisor calls, status, outcome, cost) and `advisor_cost_usd` (advisor spend outside the totals). The crew job's MCP server gets `--advisor` on its command line. Prune removes `<state>/advisor/<id>`.
